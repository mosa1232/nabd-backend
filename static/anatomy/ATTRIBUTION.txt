Anatomy data (bp3d.bin, bp3d.json)

Derived from Z-Anatomy, "the libre 3D atlas of anatomy" (Gauthier Kervyn - design, 3D, anatomy;
Marcin Zielinski - Blender add-on; Lluis Vinent - Unity), licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy (Z-Anatomy.zip -> Startup.blend, TA2.csv)

Z-Anatomy is itself built on BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan
(https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html), and uses Terminologia Anatomica
terms (Latin names from Z-Anatomy's TA2 table).

Changes made: the anatomical meshes and bevelled curves (vessels, nerves) were evaluated in Blender
and exported; helper objects, title text, reference planes, body-region overlays and muscle
origin/insertion overlays were left out; thinned by vertex clustering (9.7M -> 2.9M triangles);
positions quantised to 16 bits per axis inside each structure's bounding box; coordinates converted
to Y-up in units of 10 cm; Arabic names and short descriptions added. These files remain under
CC BY-SA 4.0 (and CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan for the BodyParts3D-derived parts).

Left out on purpose because their licences are not compatible with this use or are unclear:
 - the kidney (Lissie Cowley, CC BY-NC) and the inner-ear structures (University of Dundee, CC BY-NC-SA),
   both non-commercial;
 - the white-matter tracts and fibre bundles ("Brainder" / University of Washington), licence unstated.
