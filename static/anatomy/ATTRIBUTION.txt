Changes made: elements grouped into named anatomical parts (most specific concept),
classified into body systems, coordinates rotated to Y-up, meshes thinned by vertex
clustering (6.7M -> 2.15M polygons), positions quantised to 16 bits per axis inside each
part's bounding box, packed into one binary with a JSON catalogue. Arabic names and
descriptions were added. These files remain under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.
